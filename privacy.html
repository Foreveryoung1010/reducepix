Privacy notice

Privacy, in plain language.

Last reviewed: August 24, 2026. This notice describes the current behavior of ReducePix's static website and browser-based image compressor. It is not legal advice.

Image processing

Images are processed entirely in your browser. Your image content is never sent to any server. When you use the compressor, your browser reads selected files from your device and processes them with the Canvas API. ReducePix does not upload, store or receive image pixels, filenames or image metadata through the compressor. Compressed files remain in the page session until you download them or close the tab.

Website delivery

ReducePix is deployed on Cloudflare infrastructure. Cloudflare may process standard technical request data, such as an IP address, browser details and request time, to deliver, secure and operate the site. This is separate from image processing. Review Cloudflare's current privacy policy for its infrastructure practices.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current ReducePix page does not intentionally set application cookies and does not include analytics, advertising or third-party tracking scripts. This may change only after this notice and any required consent experience are updated.

Your choices and requests

You can stop all image processing by closing the page. No ReducePix account is created. Before public launch, the site operator must publish a monitored privacy-request contact address and update this section with the operator identity, contact details, retention information and any jurisdiction-specific disclosures required for the audience being served.

Changes

When the site's data practices change, this page and its review date should be updated before the change is promoted.
